( 07 )  Questions

Asked by
partners.

How is your fee structured?+

Permanent hires are on a success fee — you pay only when a candidate joins. Season staffing is billed monthly per professional. Executive search uses a retained model.

Do you verify ICAI and ICSI membership?+

Yes. Membership and articleship details are checked before any profile reaches you, and the result is noted on the shortlist.

Can you staff only for busy season?+

Yes. Contract professionals for 3–6 months across return, tax-audit and AGM season, with payroll and compliance handled by us.

Which cities do you cover?+

We recruit across India, with recruiters based in Bengaluru and Hyderabad. Remote and hybrid roles are welcome.

What if a hire leaves early?+

If a permanent hire leaves within 90 days, we run the search again at no additional fee.
